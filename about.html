About SoLuna

Our story, our purpose, our sound

Where Sun Meets Moon

SoLuna — a union of Sol (sun) and Luna (moon) — was born from a deep belief that healing lives in the space between opposites: stillness and vibration, silence and sound, light and dark.

We create immersive community sound bath experiences using crystal harp, gongs, chakra bells, handpan, and Native American flute. Each session is a carefully crafted journey designed to guide you into deep relaxation, helping you release stress and reconnect with yourself.

Sound healing uses vibrations and frequencies to bring the mind and body into balance. Many participants feel lighter, more peaceful, and deeply refreshed after a session. Whether you're seeking stress relief, emotional release, or simply a moment of peace in a busy world, SoLuna offers a sacred space for your healing journey.
