Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to a sound bath?

Just yourself! We provide yoga mats, blankets, and pillows. Feel free to bring your own mat or blanket for extra comfort. Wear comfortable clothing and arrive 10 minutes early.

Do I need any experience?

Not at all! Sound baths are accessible to everyone, regardless of experience. There's nothing you need to "do" — simply lie down, close your eyes, and let the sound wash over you.

How is a sound bath different from music therapy?

While both use sound, a sound bath is a meditative, immersive experience focused on resonance and vibration rather than structured music. The frequencies work on a cellular level to promote deep relaxation and healing.

Can I book a private session for someone else?

Absolutely! Gift certificates are available for all our services. It's a beautiful and unique gift for someone you love. Contact us to arrange a gift certificate.
